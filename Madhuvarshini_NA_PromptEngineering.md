# Part 1: Prompt Design & Iteration

## 1.1 Scenario A: Technical Explanation
#### Version 1 Prompt
Explain what neural networks are to a 10-year-old.

#### Version 1 Output
A neural network is a computer system inspired by the human brain. It learns from examples and helps computers recognize patterns, such as identifying pictures or understanding speech.

#### Version 2 Prompt
Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old using a simple real-life example.

#### Version 2 Output
Imagine you are learning to recognize different dog breeds. At first, you make guesses, but after seeing many dogs, you get better at identifying them. A neural network learns in a similar way by looking at lots of examples and improving over time.

#### Version 3 Prompt
You are an expert elementary school teacher.

Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old using simple language, a relatable example, and a short analogy. Avoid technical jargon. End with one fun fact.

#### Final Output
Think about how you learned to recognize your friends at school. At first, you looked at things like their hair, height, or clothes. After seeing them many times, your brain became very good at recognizing them quickly.

A neural network works in a similar way. It is a computer system that learns by looking at many examples. For example, if it sees thousands of pictures of cats and dogs, it learns what makes a cat different from a dog.

You can think of a neural network as a team of tiny helpers inside a computer. Each helper looks at a small clue, and together they decide on the answer.

Fun fact: Neural networks help power things like voice assistants, translation apps, and image recognition systems.

| V2 | Added request for real-life example | Included example | Made concept easier to understand |
| V3 | Added role, analogy, simple language, fun fact | More context and constraints | Output became more engaging and age-appropriate |

## 1.1 Scenario B: Professional Email

#### Version 1 Prompt
Write a professional email declining a job offer politely while expressing interest in working with the company in the future.

#### Version 1 Output
Subject: Thank You for the Opportunity

Dear Hiring Manager,

Thank you for offering me the position. After careful consideration, I have decided to decline the offer at this time.

I appreciate the opportunity and hope to stay in touch regarding future opportunities.

Best regards,
Madhuvarshini

#### Version 2 Prompt
Write a professional and polite email declining a job offer. Thank the company for the opportunity, briefly mention that I have decided to pursue another opportunity, and express interest in staying connected for future roles.

#### Version 2 Output
Subject: Thank You for the Job Offer

Dear Hiring Manager,

Thank you very much for offering me the position. I sincerely appreciate the time and effort invested throughout the recruitment process.

After careful consideration, I have decided to pursue another opportunity that aligns more closely with my current career goals.

I was impressed by the company and would welcome the opportunity to stay connected for future roles.

Thank you again for your understanding.

Kind regards,
Madhuvarshini

#### Version 3 Prompt
You are a professional HR communication specialist.

Context:
I recently received a job offer from a company that I admire. However, I have decided to accept another opportunity that better matches my current goals. I want to decline respectfully while maintaining a positive relationship with the company.

Write a professional email that:
- Politely declines the offer
- Shows gratitude
- Explains the decision briefly
- Expresses continued interest in the company
- Maintains a warm and professional tone

#### Final Output
Subject: Thank You for the Opportunity

Dear Hiring Manager,

I would like to sincerely thank you for offering me the opportunity to join your organization. I greatly appreciate the time, effort, and consideration extended to me throughout the recruitment process.

After careful thought, I have decided to accept another opportunity that aligns more closely with my current career objectives. This was not an easy decision, as I hold your organization in high regard and was genuinely impressed by the team and company culture.

I am grateful for your confidence in my abilities and hope to stay connected for potential opportunities in the future. I wish the team continued success and hope our paths may cross again.

Thank you once again for your understanding.

Kind regards,

Madhuvarshini N A
