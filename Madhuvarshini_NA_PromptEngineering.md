# Part 1: Prompt Design & Iteration

## 1.1 Scenario A: Technical Explanation
#### Version 1 Prompt
Explain what neural networks are to a 10-year-old.

#### Version 1 Output
A neural network is a computer system inspired by the human brain. It learns from examples and helps computers recognize patterns, such as identifying pictures or understanding speech.

#### Version 2 Prompt
Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old using a simple real-life example.

#### Version 2 Output
Imagine you are learning to recognize different dog breeds. At first, you make guesses, but after seeing many dogs, you get better at identifying them. A neural network learns in a similar way by looking at lots of examples and improving over time.

#### Version 3 Prompt
You are an expert elementary school teacher.

Explain neural networks to a 10-year-old using simple language, a relatable example, and a short analogy. Avoid technical jargon. End with one fun fact.

#### Final Output
Think about how you learned to recognize your friends at school. At first, you looked at things like their hair, height, or clothes. After seeing them many times, your brain became very good at recognizing them quickly.

A neural network works in a similar way. It is a computer system that learns by looking at many examples. For example, if it sees thousands of pictures of cats and dogs, it learns what makes a cat different from a dog.

You can think of a neural network as a team of tiny helpers inside a computer. Each helper looks at a small clue, and together they decide on the answer.

Fun fact: Neural networks help power things like voice assistants, translation apps, and image recognition systems.
